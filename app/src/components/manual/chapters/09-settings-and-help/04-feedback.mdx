You can report a bug or suggest an idea from inside <App />, and read replies there too. Open **Settings → Feedback** and press **Send feedback**, or click **Report bugs here** on the home screen.

<Img name="feedback.png" caption="The feedback form: what happened, what to attach, and a preview of exactly what will be sent." />

## Sending a report

- **Kind.** **Something is broken** or **Suggestion**. Bug reports also have a **Steps to reproduce** box.
- **Images.** Attach screenshots by browsing, dragging them in, or pasting.
- **Attachments.** Your app version and plugins, the settings you've changed, and the recent log. On by default for bugs, off for suggestions.
- **Preview.** **Show exactly what will be sent** shows the whole report before you send it.

<Note>

Reports become public issues on the project's GitHub. Your username is removed from file paths, but don't include anything you wouldn't want public.

</Note>

You can report anonymously, or sign in with GitHub to file it under your account and get replies there too.

## Replies

Your reports are listed in **Settings → Feedback**. A dot on the Settings gear means someone replied. Open a report to read and answer; <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Enter</Kbd> sends.

Anonymous replies only come back to this computer.
