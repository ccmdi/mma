export const title = "Commits & diffs";

A **Commit** saves your current changes as a version of the map. Unlike autosave, it's a checkpoint you can come back to.

- **The Commit button.** In the bottom bar, greyed out when there's nothing to commit. A click (or <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>S</Kbd>) commits right away. <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+click first shows what's changed and lets you add a **message**, which labels the commit in the history.
- **The changes.** Next to the button: **+added**, **-removed**, and **±modified** since the last commit. The **Uncommitted** selection shows which locations those are.

Committing clears the undo history. Past commits are in <ChapterLink id="version-history">History</ChapterLink> in the bottom bar.
