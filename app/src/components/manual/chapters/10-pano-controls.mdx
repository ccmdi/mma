Overlay controls sit on top of the panorama. Which ones appear is up to you: **Settings → Street View → Viewer controls** has a switch per control.

<Img name="pano-controls.png" caption="The panorama overlay: compass, zoom, return-to-spawn, jump, coordinates, and map links." />

## Orientation and movement

- **Compass.** A wind rose that rotates with your heading; click it to point north, or <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>-click to cycle through linked panoramas at the same location. An optional heading tape is also available. Point north with <Kbd>N</Kbd>, spin 180 with <Kbd>T</Kbd>, and face along the nearest road with <Kbd>B</Kbd>.
- **Zoom.** Zoom the panorama in and out (<Kbd>+</Kbd> / <Kbd>-</Kbd>). Press <Kbd>0</Kbd> to zoom all the way out.
- **Return to spawn.** Snaps the pano back to the location's saved coordinates and clears any per-location pano pin (<Kbd>R</Kbd>). <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>R</Kbd> just reloads the current panorama.
- **Jump forward / backward.** Hops roughly 100m along the road (<Kbd>{"}"}</Kbd> / <Kbd>{"{"}</Kbd>).
- **Follow road.** Walks along linked panos in the direction you face and adds each one as a new location (<Kbd>G</Kbd>).
- **Look and walk from the keyboard.** Arrow keys look around; <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>Up</Kbd> / <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>Down</Kbd> walk forward and back.

## Reference and links

- **Altitude / zoom display.** Shows the current pano's altitude (when available) and zoom level.
- **Pano metadata.** An optional corner readout of whether the location is pinned to a pano, plus every metadata field as a save would write it - it follows the pano you are viewing, not just what is stored. Handy while enriching or auditing.
- **Map links.** Open in Google Maps and copy a Street View link (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>C</Kbd>). Hold <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> while copying to omit tags from the URL, or <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> to copy the full long URL instead of a short link.
- **Download panorama.** Stitches and saves the whole panorama image (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>S</Kbd>). This is the full sphere, not what you are looking at.
- **Screenshot.** Captures the current view as a 1920x1080 PNG and copies it to the clipboard, ready to paste elsewhere. Hold <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> to save it as a file instead.
- **Pan map to location.** <Kbd>L</Kbd> centers the map on whatever you are looking at, which is useful once you have walked away from the marker.

## Display options

- **Fullscreen.** Expands the panorama and shows a **minimap** and a **tag bar** (each toggleable) (<Kbd>F</Kbd>). The minimap mirrors the editor map, rendering all markers, selections, and polygons, and follows the active location. The date picker, review bar, and geocoding info can each be shown in fullscreen too, from **Settings → Street View → Fullscreen**.
- **Hide car.** Masks the capture vehicle (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>H</Kbd>).
- **Crosshair.** A center crosshair for precise framing (<Kbd>X</Kbd>).
- **Toggle pano UI.** Hides the overlay controls for a clean view (<Kbd>H</Kbd>). By default this also hides the navigation arrows and click-to-go markers; turn that off in **Settings → Street View → Navigation**.
- **Preview aspect ratio.** The panorama pane can be shaped to a fixed aspect ratio (4:3, 16:10, 16:9, 21:9, 32:9) to match how the map will be played, or left **Free** to fill the pane. Set it in **Settings → Street View**.
- **Lock viewport direction.** <Kbd>V</Kbd> keeps your framing fixed relative to each panorama's own camera as you move between them, rather than snapping to each one's default view.

## Movement modes and speed

- **Movement mode.** The default is set in Settings: **Moving** (walk and look freely), **No Move** (look only, no walking), or **NMPZ** (no move, pan, or zoom). <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>M</Kbd> cycles through the three.
- **Look & pan speed.** Pano look speed is adjustable in **Settings → Street View**, and map pan speed in **Settings → Map → Navigation**. Hold <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> for the slow modifier (its divisor is configurable in the same place).
