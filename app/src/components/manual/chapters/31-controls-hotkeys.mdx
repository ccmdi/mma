The **Keyboard** section of Settings holds every rebindable shortcut, grouped by where it applies: Commands, Global, Location Editor, Map Navigation, Review, and Quicktag. Every command in the palette can be given a key here, not just the built-in actions. Movement speeds live elsewhere: map pan speed and the Alt slow-down in the **Map** section, pano look speed in the **Street View** section.

## Rebinding keys

- **Click to rebind.** Click a shortcut's current binding, then press the new key combination. Press <Kbd>Backspace</Kbd> or <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> while recording to clear it, or <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> to cancel.
- **Filter.** A search box narrows the (long) shortcut list.
- **Reset.** Each customized binding shows a **Reset** button; there is also **Reset all to defaults**.

## Conflict resolution

If you assign a combination already in use, <App /> shows what it is bound to and offers to **Reassign** (which clears it from the other action). Conflicting rows are flagged with a warning you can click to jump to the other action. Some combinations are blocked: for example, a combination using <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> cannot shadow a navigation action, because <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> is the slow modifier.

## Per-map hotkeys

Each map can have its own hotkeys that override global bindings while the map is open. Two kinds of per-map binding are available:

- **Tag hotkeys.** Assign a key to a tag from its edit dialog. Pressing the key while a location is open toggles the tag.
- **Copy-to-map.** Assign a key to another map. Pressing the key copies the open location into that map.

## Movement speeds

- **Pan speed.** How fast the map pans with the keyboard (**Settings → Map → Navigation**).
- **Pan to imported locations.** Whether the map jumps to newly imported locations (**Settings → Map → Navigation**).
- **Pano look speed.** How fast the panorama camera turns with the keyboard (**Settings → Street View**).
- **Alt slow-down.** The divisor applied while you hold <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> for fine control (**Settings → Map → Navigation**).
