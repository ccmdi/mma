export const title = "Commits & diffs";

A **Commit** bakes the current working changes into a permanent version. It is the durable checkpoint, distinct from the always-on autosave.

- **The Commit button.** In the bottom bar. Disabled when there is nothing to commit (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>S</Kbd>). A plain click commits right away. <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+click opens a dialog showing the pending diff and an optional **message** describing the change, which then labels that commit in the history.
- **The pending diff.** Beside the button: **+added**, **-removed**, and **±modified** counts since the last commit.

<Note>

Undo/redo operates on individual edits within your session. Commits are the browsable, durable checkpoints. Use undo for "oops", use commits for milestones. Committing clears the undo/redo history. Browse past commits from the **History** button in the bottom bar (see <ChapterLink id="version-history" />).

</Note>
