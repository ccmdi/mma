The bar along the bottom of the editor.

<Img name="bottom-bar.png" caption="The bottom bar: location count, Commit with diff, undo/redo, Seen, History, Import, Export." />

- **Location count.** How many locations the map has.
- **<ChapterLink id="commits">Commit</ChapterLink>.** Saves your changes as a version. Next to it, **+added**, **-removed**, and **±modified** count what's changed since the last commit. <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+click to add a message.
- **<ChapterLink id="saving">Undo / Redo</ChapterLink>.** Step back and forward through your edits (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Z</Kbd> / <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Y</Kbd>).
- **<ChapterLink id="seen">Seen</ChapterLink>.** Every panorama you've looked at.
- **<ChapterLink id="version-history">History</ChapterLink>.** This map's commits.
- **<ChapterLink id="importing-into-map">Import file</ChapterLink>.** Adds locations from a file.
- **<ChapterLink id="exporting-map">Export</ChapterLink>.** Saves the map, or the selection, to a file.
