There are two fullscreen modes: the panorama (<Kbd>F</Kbd>) and the map (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>{"\\"}</Kbd>). Only one can be on at a time, and <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> leaves either.

## Fullscreen panorama

With a location open, <Kbd>F</Kbd> fills the window with the panorama. A minimap and tag bar sit on top, so you can keep tagging and placing locations. Hover the minimap to make it bigger.

**Settings → Street View → Fullscreen panorama** sets what shows: the minimap, tag bar, date picker, review bar, and address.

## Fullscreen map

<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>{"\\"}</Kbd> hides the sidebar so the map fills the window. An open location shows as a small preview in the corner. It has the panorama but no buttons, so use <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> to save, <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> to close, and <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> to delete.

**Settings → Map → Fullscreen map** sets whether the bottom bar and the preview show.
