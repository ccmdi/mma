The **Map style** button (top-left of the map) changes what's under your markers: the basemap, the Street View coverage lines, and borders. These apply to every map, not just the open one.

## The map style panel

- **Basemap.** **Map**, **Satellite**, **OSM** (OpenStreetMap), or **Vector**. <Kbd>J</Kbd> and <Kbd>K</Kbd> switch between them.
- **Layers.** **Terrain**, **Labels**, and **Panoramas** (Street View dots, when zoomed in close). The Street View coverage layer is always on.
- **Street View.** Which coverage lines to show (**Official**, **Unofficial**, or **All**), their color, thinner lines, and a blobby layer when zoomed out.
- **Settings.** Stronger country and subdivision borders, and switches to hide road labels, points of interest, transit, and highways.
- **Map style.** **Default**, **Legacy** (the classic GeoGuessr look), and a range of muted, dark, and high-contrast styles. Add your own with **Manage map styles**.

**Vector** has its own style list and doesn't use the options above.

## Layer opacity

The slider in the top-right fades one layer at a time. The buttons next to it pick which: Street View coverage, unselected markers, or selected markers (only there while something is selected). A button is crossed out while its layer is hidden.

<Kbd>P</Kbd> hides and shows the Street View layer, and <Kbd>M</Kbd> the unselected markers.
