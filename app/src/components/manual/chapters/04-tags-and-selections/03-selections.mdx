A selection is a group of your locations, highlighted on the map in its own color. The Selections section lists the active ones with how many locations they match. They update as you edit.

Anything that works on many locations at once (reviewing, exporting, bulk operations, deleting) works on what's selected.

<Img name="selections.png" caption="The Selections section: each rule drawn in its own color, with a per-selection menu." />

## Kinds of selections

- **<ChapterLink id="tags">Tag</ChapterLink>** and **Untagged.** Locations with a tag, or with none. Click a tag in the sidebar to select it.
- **Unpanned.** Locations you haven't saved a view for yet.
- **Pano ID locations** / **Coordinate locations.** By <ChapterLink id="loading-flags">how they load</ChapterLink>.
- **Uncommitted.** Added or changed since the last commit.
- **Polygon.** An area you draw with the buttons on the left of the map: polygon, rectangle, or freehand. Click the first point or double-click to finish; <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> cancels. Hold <Kbd>E</Kbd> and click a polygon to remove it.
- **Country / Subdivision.** Hold <Kbd>Q</Kbd> and click the map for a country, <Kbd>Q</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd> for a state or province. Subdivisions need **Subdivision data** turned on in **Settings → Map → Borders**.
- **<ChapterLink id="duplicates">Duplicates</ChapterLink>.** Locations within a distance of each other.
- **<ChapterLink id="validation">Validation</ChapterLink>.** Problems found by validation.
- **<ChapterLink id="reviewing">Reviewed / Unreviewed</ChapterLink>.** The two halves of a review.
- **<ChapterLink id="filters">Filter</ChapterLink>.** By a field's value, e.g. altitude above 500m.
- **Top / Bottom K.** The highest or lowest K locations by a field.
- **Manual.** Locations you <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>-click one by one.
- **Everything.** <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>A</Kbd>. <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>D</Kbd> clears all selections.
- **GeoJSON shapes.** **Load shapes from GeoJSON as selection** turns shapes from a `.geojson` file into polygons.

Selections can be <ChapterLink id="selection-algebra">combined</ChapterLink>, narrowed to a <ChapterLink id="sampling">random or spread-out sample</ChapterLink>, and <ChapterLink id="saved-selections">saved</ChapterLink> for other maps.

## The selection menu

Click a selection's name to zoom to it. The dots button has:

- **Invert selection.** Everything except this.
- **Edit filter.** For filter selections.
- **Review selection.** Go through it in a <ChapterLink id="reviewing">review</ChapterLink>.
- **Save as tag.** Puts a new tag on everything in it.
- **Prune duplicates.** On a duplicates selection, deletes the extras.
- **Change color.** Tag selections use the tag's color instead.
- **Download GeoJSON** and **Rename.** For polygons.
- **Deselect.**

To tag everything selected, type into **Bulk-add tag** at the end of the toolbar.

## Ghosting

The ghost button on a selection dims it on the map without removing it. <Kbd>Alt</Kbd>-click it to ghost every other selection instead, and <Kbd>Alt</Kbd>-click again to bring them back.

A ghosted selection is left out of anything that acts on "the selection" (delete, bulk operations, Bulk-add tag), so you can keep it around without it getting in the way.

## Undoing selection changes

Selections have their own undo, separate from your edits: <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>D</Kbd>, and add <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> to redo. It lasts while the map is open.
