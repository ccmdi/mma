A filter selects locations by a field's value, e.g. everything above 500m altitude, or everything from 2023. Make one with **Filter by metadata...** in the command palette or toolbar. To change it later, use **Edit filter** on its menu.

<Img name="filter.png" caption="The filter form: pick a field, an operator, and a value." />

Filters only see metadata that's already on your locations, so <ChapterLink id="enrichment">enrich</ChapterLink> first.

## Fields

- **Built-in.** **Created**, **Modified**, **Pano ID**, **Load as pano ID**, **Tags**, **Tag count**, **Heading**, **Pitch**, and **Zoom**.
- **Metadata.** Any field on the map, like **Country code**, **Camera type**, **Altitude**, **Image date**, and your own fields. What you can ask depends on the field's <ChapterLink id="managing-fields">type</ChapterLink>.

## Operators

- **= / !=** equals and not equals.
- **&gt; / &lt; / &gt;= / &lt;=** for numbers and dates.
- **between** a range. Date and month fields can tick **Any year**, e.g. June to August in any year. Exact dates can tick **Any date** to match a time of day instead.
- **has / does not have** a value at all.
- **contains / does not contain** for lists like tags, plus comparisons on their length.

Fields with a fixed set of values (like camera type) only have the equals operators, with a list to pick from.

## Dates and timezones

Dates are compared in your own timezone by default. Tick **Location timezone** on an exact-date filter to use each location's local time instead, which matters when filtering by time of day across countries. Locations with no timezone are skipped, and you're told how many.

Exact-date filters pick whole days, matching how dates are shown. With **Exact date format** set to **Date + time** (**Settings → Street View → Date picker**), they get a time box too.

## Stepping through ranges

Filter selections with a range have left/right arrows. They move the range forward or back by its own size, so you can step through dates day by day, or altitudes band by band.

To ask for more than one thing at once (Gen 4 _and_ above 500m), <ChapterLink id="selection-algebra">combine filters</ChapterLink>.
