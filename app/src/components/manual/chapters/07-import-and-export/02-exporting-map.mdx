**Export** in the bottom bar saves the map, or just the selection, to a file.

<Img name="export.png" caption="The export dialog: scope, JSON format and options, then CSV and GeoJSON." />

## JSON (recommended)

Copy it or download it. **Format** picks how much goes in, depending on where it's going:

- **GeoGuessr.** Coordinates, views, and pinned pano IDs. The smallest file, for uploading.
- **map-making.app.** Adds tags, unpinned pano IDs, and capture months. No custom fields.
- **Local.** Everything, including custom fields and doclinks. Use this for moving a map between <App /> installs.

Options:

- **Save zoom levels.** Include each location's zoom.
- **Bypass GeoGuessr auto-panning for locations with 0 heading.** Keeps unpanned locations unpanned in the game.

The format and options are remembered per map.

## CSV and GeoJSON

- **CSV.** Coordinates only (`lat,lng`): no views, pano IDs, tags, or metadata. For handing coordinates to another tool, not for moving a map.
- **GeoJSON.** For other mapping tools. Each point keeps its tag names.

To export every map at once, use <ChapterLink id="bulk-backup">Export all maps</ChapterLink> on the home screen.
