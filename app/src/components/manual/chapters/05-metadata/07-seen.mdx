<App /> keeps a history of every panorama you look at, across all maps. It's useful for going back to somewhere you've been, and for seeing where you've already looked.

## Recording

It's on by default. **Settings → Editing → Seen** turns it off, and sets whether thumbnails are saved and at what size (bigger thumbnails take more space).

## Browsing

**Seen** in the bottom bar opens the history: a thumbnail, flag, and address for each visit. Click one to open it. You can filter by country, by map, or by searching the address.

It keeps the most recent 10,000, dropping the oldest. **Clear** wipes it.

## On the map

**Toggle seen locations overlay** (command palette) draws every pano you've seen as a dot on the map. Blue dots are already on this map, and clicking opens the location. Orange dots are only in your history, and clicking previews the pano with the option to add it. Nothing is added until you say so.

It's the quick way to see where you've already been while filling in an area: the orange dots are the ones you looked at and didn't keep.
