Opening a map launches the editor: a resizable split between the **map** and the **sidebar**. Drag the divider between them to rebalance the panes (its position is remembered).

## The sidebar

With no location open, the sidebar shows two sections:

- **Tags.** Create, color, and assign labels. A filter input and sort buttons (default, name, amount) sit at the top.
- **Selections.** Pick subsets of locations and combine them. Its header bar includes the plugin toolbar and a **Commands...** button that opens the command palette (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>K</Kbd>). Below the active selections is a pinnable toolbar holding shortcut buttons for commands - right-click it to add, remove, or reorder them. Some of them (find duplicates, filter by metadata, the random and spaced pickers, top/bottom K) open their form inline in the toolbar rather than a dialog.

Clicking a location's marker swaps the sidebar for the location editor (covered later). The arrow at the top-left of the header goes back to the map list, as does <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>W</Kbd>.

## Fullscreen map

<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>{"\\"}</Kbd> hides the sidebar and gives the map the whole window. The location editor becomes a small floating preview in the corner instead of disappearing, so you can keep editing. What stays visible is configurable in **Settings → Map → Fullscreen**: the bottom bar and the mini location preview can each be turned off. Fullscreen map and fullscreen panorama (<Kbd>F</Kbd>) are mutually exclusive - entering one suspends the other and leaving it restores what you had.

## Basemap and layers

Click the basemap control (top-left of the map) to choose the base imagery and open the layers panel:

- **Basemap.** **Map**, **Satellite**, **OSM** (OpenStreetMap), or **Vector**.
- **Layers.** **Terrain**, **Labels**, and **Panoramas** (individual Street View dots, requires close zoom). The base Street View layer is always on.
- **Street View.** Coverage lines: **Show lines** filtered to **Official**, **Unofficial**, or **All**; a color swatch for the lines; **Make the lines thinner**; and **Use blobby layer while zoomed out**.
- **Settings.** **Emphasise country borders**, **Emphasise subdivision borders**, and switches to hide **road labels**, **points of interest**, **transit**, and **highways**.
- **Map style.** **Default**, **Legacy** (the classic GeoGuessr style), **Muted**, **Midnight**, **Minimal**, **Vintage**, **Grayscale**, **Blueprint**, **Arctic**, **Ember**, **Forest**, **Noir**, **Dusk**, any custom styles you add, and a **Manage map styles** link.

The **Vector** basemap has its own style list (Liberty, Bright, Positron, Fiord (dark), Dark Matter, Voyager, Eclipse (dark), Colorful) and does not take the Google styling options above.

## Select-only mode

Toggle **Select-only mode** (<Kbd>O</Kbd>) from the Map settings dropdown or its hotkey. When active, clicking the map never creates a new location; only existing markers are clickable. Useful when you want to browse and select without accidentally adding pins.

## Navigating the map

- **Pan / zoom.** Mouse drag and wheel, or the keyboard: <Kbd>W</Kbd><Kbd>A</Kbd><Kbd>S</Kbd><Kbd>D</Kbd> to pan and <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>W</Kbd> / <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>S</Kbd> to zoom. Hold <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> for slow, fine movement. <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>B</Kbd> fits the map to all locations, <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>E</Kbd> to the selected ones, and <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>0</Kbd> zooms all the way out. All of these are rebindable.
- **Place search.** The search control geocodes a place name via Nominatim and fits the map to it.
- **Opacity slider.** A slider in the top-right controls the opacity of the Street View coverage layer or the markers - click the icon next to it to switch between the two. <Kbd>P</Kbd> and <Kbd>M</Kbd> toggle each layer straight to hidden and back.
