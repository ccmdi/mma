Review mode goes through a selection one location at a time, and keeps track of which ones you've already looked at. You can stop and pick up where you left off.

## Review mode

Start it with **Review selection** on a <ChapterLink id="selections">selection</ChapterLink>'s menu, or **Review selected locations** in the command palette (also on the toolbar).

- **Next / Previous.** <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Right</Kbd> and <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Left</Kbd>. **Save** also moves on and marks the location reviewed.
- **Skip.** <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> moves on without saving, and still marks it reviewed.
- **Delete.** Deletes the location and moves on.
- **Exit review.** The X in the review bar. Your progress is kept. The bar shows where you are (**3 / 50**) and how many you've reviewed.

Clicking a marker that's in the review jumps there. Clicking one that isn't just opens it without losing your place.

## Picking up where you left off

Running **Review selection** on the same selection again continues the review instead of starting over. **Review sessions** lists all of them.

<Img name="review-sessions.png" caption="The review sessions dialog: in-progress and completed reviews with progress bars." />

- **In progress / Completed.** Two tabs.
- **Resume.** Continue from where you stopped.
- **Select reviewed / Select unreviewed.** The two circle buttons select the locations you have or haven't reviewed yet.
- **Rename** by clicking the name, or **Delete**.

**Select reviewed locations** in the command palette selects everything you've ever reviewed on the map, across all sessions.

While reviewing, the **Reviewed** and **Unreviewed** selections show your progress on the map.

## Review order

By default a review goes in the order the selection lists them. To see the most important ones first, set a **Review order** in **Edit map** (the pencil by the map name). It's a small <ChapterLink id="expressions">formula</ChapterLink> that scores each location, highest first. `tagCount`, for example, puts the most-tagged locations first.
