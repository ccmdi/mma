The panorama also has buttons for copying links, taking screenshots, and changing how it looks. Like the other <ChapterLink id="pano-controls">pano controls</ChapterLink>, each can be turned off in **Settings → Street View → Viewer controls**.

## Links and images

- **Map links.** Open the view in Google Maps, or copy a link to it (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>C</Kbd>). Hold <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> to leave the tags out, or <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> for the long URL instead of a short one.
- **Screenshot.** Copies what you're looking at as a 1920x1080 image. Hold <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> to save it as a file instead.
- **Download panorama** (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>S</Kbd>). Saves the whole panorama, not just your view. For many at once, use <ChapterLink id="bulk-operations">Download panoramas</ChapterLink>.

## Info

- **Altitude and zoom.** Shown in the corner.
- **Pano metadata.** An optional readout of whether the location is pinned, plus the metadata a save would write. Handy while enriching or checking a location.

## Display

- **Crosshair** (<Kbd>X</Kbd>) in the middle of the view.
- **Hide car** (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>H</Kbd>).
- **Hide pano UI** (<Kbd>H</Kbd>) for a clean view. By default this hides the road arrows too.
- **Aspect ratio.** Shape the panorama like the game will (4:3 up to 32:9), or **Free** to fill the space. Set in **Settings → Street View**.
- **Lock view** (<Kbd>V</Kbd>). Keeps your view the same relative to the car as you move between panoramas, instead of resetting at each one.

<Kbd>F</Kbd> opens the <ChapterLink id="fullscreen">fullscreen panorama</ChapterLink>.
