Most places have been photographed more than once. The date picker under the panorama lists every capture of the pano you're looking at, so you can choose which one the location shows.

- **Pick a date** to load that capture. <Kbd>[</Kbd> and <Kbd>]</Kbd> step through them.
- **Default** loads whatever the coordinates show, and keeps up as new coverage comes out.
- **Camera badges** show each capture's camera generation. Turn them off in **Settings → Street View → Date picker**. Unofficial coverage is always marked.

If every capture of a pano is unofficial, the official captures right next to it are listed too.

More on camera generations in [this guide](https://www.plonkit.net/beginners-guide#:~:text=Camera%20Generation).

## Pinning a date

Picking a date <ChapterLink id="loading-flags">pins</ChapterLink> that exact pano to the location. **Default** or **Return to spawn** unpins it again.

Once a location has an <ChapterLink id="enrichment">exact date</ChapterLink>, the picker shows that instead of just the month.
