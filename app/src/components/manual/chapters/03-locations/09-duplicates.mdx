export const title = "Duplicates & deleting";

Maps pick up duplicates: the same pano added twice, two locations a few meters apart, an import that overlaps what you had. There are tools for each.

## Several locations at one spot

Clicking a marker with other locations within two meters of it doesn't open the panorama. The sidebar lists everything at that spot instead, each with a thumbnail and its tags.

- **Click a thumbnail** to open that location.
- **Delete** on a row deletes just that one.
- Tick rows, then **Keep selected** (deletes the others) or **Delete selected**.

This is the way out of accidentally adding the same pano twice.

## Across the whole map

**Find duplicates...** selects every location with another one within a distance you choose. From there, two tools that are easy to mix up:

- **Merge duplicates...** collapses each group into one location, and puts all their tags on the one that's left. Works on the whole map.
- **Prune duplicates** (on the Duplicates selection's menu) deletes the extras instead, and only touches that selection. Under 25m it keeps the best location in each group; above that it thins them out to that spacing.

Use Merge when the duplicates have tags you want to keep, and Prune when you just want them gone.

Both keep whichever location scores highest on the map's **Duplicate preference** (in **Edit map**). By default that's the one with the most tags, a pinned pano, and a saved view; ties go to the oldest. You can write your own as an <ChapterLink id="expressions">expression</ChapterLink>.

## Duplicating on purpose

**Duplicate location** in the <ChapterLink id="map-context-menu">right-click menu</ChapterLink> adds a copy at the same spot. To add another view further down the road, use <ChapterLink id="adding-locations">Drop marker</ChapterLink> instead.

## Deleting

- **One.** **Delete** or <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> in the location editor.
- **Many.** Select them, then <Kbd>Delete</Kbd> on the map, or **Delete selected locations** from the command palette or toolbar.

<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Z</Kbd> brings deleted locations back.
