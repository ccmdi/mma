The controls on the panorama for looking around and moving. Each one can be turned off in **Settings → Street View → Viewer controls**. The rest of the overlay (<ChapterLink id="pano-tools">links, screenshots, and display options</ChapterLink>) has its own chapter.

<Img name="pano-controls.png" caption="The panorama overlay: compass, zoom, return-to-spawn, jump, coordinates, and map links." />

## Looking around

- **Compass.** Turns with your view. Click it to face north, or <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>-click to turn toward each road.
- **Face north** (<Kbd>N</Kbd>). Press it again while facing north to look straight down.
- **Turn around** (<Kbd>T</Kbd>), or face along the road (<Kbd>B</Kbd>).
- **Zoom.** <Kbd>+</Kbd> / <Kbd>-</Kbd>, and <Kbd>0</Kbd> to zoom all the way out.
- **Arrow keys** look around.

## Moving

- **Walk.** Click the arrows on the road, or <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>Up</Kbd> / <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>Down</Kbd>.
- **Jump forward / backward.** About 100m along the road (<Kbd>{"}"}</Kbd> / <Kbd>{"{"}</Kbd>).
- **Return to spawn** (<Kbd>R</Kbd>). Back to where the location started, with its saved view. This also unpins the pano. <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>R</Kbd> just reloads the panorama.
- **Center the map** (<Kbd>L</Kbd>) on where you're standing, handy once you've walked away from the marker.

Walking doesn't move the location until you save. To keep it and add a new one where you're standing, use <ChapterLink id="adding-locations">Drop marker</ChapterLink> (<Kbd>C</Kbd>).

## Movement modes and speed

- **Movement mode.** **Moving** (walk and look), **No Move** (look only), or **NMPZ** (no moving, panning, or zooming). <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>+<Kbd>M</Kbd> switches between them, so you can check a location the way it'll be played. The starting mode is set in **Settings → Street View**.
- **Speed.** Look speed is in **Settings → Street View**, map pan speed in **Settings → Map → Navigation**. Hold <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> to slow both down.
