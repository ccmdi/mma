**LocalGuessr** is a GeoGuessr-style game on your own map. It's a quick way to play a map the way others will (or just for practice), and you can tag locations as you go.

## Setting up

Turn it on in the marketplace, then open it with a map open:

- **Locations.** The whole map, or the selection.
- **Mode.** **Moving**, **No move**, or **NMPZ**.
- **Rounds.** A **Fixed** number, or **Endless**.
- **Timer.** None, counting up, or counting down.
- **Streak.** Optionally count a streak by country, or by state or region.

Then press **Play**.

## Playing

Look around, put a pin on the map, and press **Guess** (or <Kbd>Space</Kbd>). You see the answer and your score; <Kbd>Space</Kbd> again moves on. When you can move, <Kbd>C</Kbd> sets a checkpoint, <Kbd>B</Kbd> goes back to it, and <Kbd>R</Kbd> goes back to the start.

The tag bar on each round tags that location on your map, and **Tag all rounds** at the end tags them all.

Games can be resumed, and **Past games** keeps your history and stats.
