export const title = "Built-in plugins";

The plugins on the **Core** tab. Turn them on in the marketplace.

## Making locations

- **<ChapterLink id="generating-locations">Map generator</ChapterLink>.** Finds Street View locations inside areas you draw.
- **<ChapterLink id="generating-locations">Vali</ChapterLink>.** Finds locations from pre-built coverage data. Much faster, where the data exists.

## Understanding a map

- **<ChapterLink id="analysis-plugins">Distribution</ChapterLink>.** How your locations are spread across countries.
- **<ChapterLink id="analysis-plugins">Disambiguate</ChapterLink>.** Which metadata fields best tell your selections apart.
- **<ChapterLink id="analysis-plugins">Gradient</ChapterLink>.** Colors locations by a field.
- **<ChapterLink id="analysis-plugins">Pivot Table</ChapterLink>.** Counts locations across two groupings.

## Syncing

- **<ChapterLink id="syncing">map-making.app sync</ChapterLink>** and **<ChapterLink id="syncing">GeoGuessr</ChapterLink>.** Keep the map in step with one on those sites.
- **<ChapterLink id="syncing">File sync</ChapterLink>.** Keeps the map up to date with a map file.

## Playing

- **<ChapterLink id="localguessr">LocalGuessr</ChapterLink>.** A guessing game on your own map.
